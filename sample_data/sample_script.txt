SCENE 12 — EXT. CANYON RIDGE — NIGHT

JACK gallops hard on horseback along a narrow cliff-edge trail, 700 feet above
the valley floor. A GUNMAN opens fire from the rocks above.

Jack DIVES from the horse as it rears — he rolls across the rocky ground and
scrambles behind a boulder. Blood on his jacket.

A WALL OF FLAME erupts as a pyrotechnic charge detonates in the dry brush
ten feet away. Jack runs through the smoke toward the canyon edge.

The HELICOPTER UNIT captures aerial coverage from 200 feet above the cliff face.

SCENE 13 — EXT. RIVER CROSSING — DAY

THE POSSE (8 riders) crosses a fast-moving river, water chest-deep on the horses.
The current is strong — riders struggle to keep control.

SCENE 14 — EXT. HIGHWAY 138 — NIGHT

A HIGH-SPEED CAR CHASE. Two vehicles weave through traffic at 90mph.
The pursuit vehicle RAMS the target car, which spins out and CRASHES
through the guardrail.

SCENE 15 — INT. SALOON — NIGHT

A BAR FIGHT breaks out. Bottles smash, chairs fly. JAKE is thrown through
a plate glass window onto the street.
